Case Study

Industrial IoT Command Center

We built an HVAC predictive maintenance platform that captures field sensor data, computes superheat and subcooling in real time, and sends actionable insights to a remote operations dashboard.

Challenge

Wireless HVAC monitoring is still underused in many industrial settings. RF noise and physical infrastructure make standard Wi-Fi unreliable, while many existing market solutions are expensive to deploy at scale.

Solution

  • Converted analog HVAC sensor signals into robust digital telemetry using a microcontroller-based edge design
  • Implemented a dual communication stack: LoRaWAN for long-range field reliability and ESP32 Wi-Fi at gateway level for low-cost backhaul
  • Designed for long battery life with deep-sleep behavior, targeting up to 2 years of operation
  • Built a dashboard that lets teams monitor superheat and subcooling remotely from the office, without constant site visits

Outcome

The prototype is complete and production planning is now underway. The system reduces avoidable field trips, enables earlier fault detection, and gives HVAC service teams a practical path to proactive maintenance instead of reactive breakdown response.
