Case Study

Industrial IoT Command Center

A dashboard concept for multi-site industrial telemetry with early risk signals and actionable maintenance visibility.

Challenge

Operators had fragmented monitoring views and delayed responses to equipment anomalies across multiple systems.

Solution

  • Single-screen command view for risk and health status
  • Alert prioritization model for faster triage
  • Role-oriented dashboard layout for engineering and operations

Outcome

Teams gained a clearer operational picture and a practical framework for scaling live monitoring workflows.
